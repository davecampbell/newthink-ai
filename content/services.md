NewThink.ai works inside your operation. Not as advisors who hand you a roadmap, but as applied AI engineers who sit with your teams, map your workflows, and deploy systems built around your specific context and data.

We approach every engagement in three phases: **Audit** (understand your workflows and identify where AI creates real value), **Build** (prototype and refine in your environment), and **Deploy** (move to production, starting with the smallest working piece and expanding from there).

Part of the job is telling you what *not* to automate. That's where the trust gets built — and where the real ROI comes from.

---

## Getting Started

Your organization knows it needs to move on AI but isn't sure where to begin. This engagement maps your current workflows, identifies the highest-value first use case, and produces a working prototype — so you can see what's actually possible in your specific operation before committing to anything larger.

*Typical commitment: Small (0.5–2 days) to Medium (3–10 days)*

---

## Custom AI Workflows

Not every AI solution is a chatbot. Some of the highest-value work is in the back office — routing, triage, synthesis, summarization, data extraction across variable inputs. This engagement designs and builds AI-augmented workflows around how your team actually operates, connecting Claude, your existing data, and your tools into a system that runs every day.

*Typical commitment: Medium (3–10 days) to Large (10+ days)*

## Claude for Small Business

Anthropic's Claude for Business is one of the most capable platforms available to small and mid-sized organizations — and most teams barely scratch the surface of what it can do. This engagement gets your organization configured, trained, and genuinely adopted: from initial setup through real use cases your team actually cares about.

*Typical commitment: Small (0.5–2 days) to Medium (3–10 days)*

## NanoClaw for Business

Some workflows call for a dedicated AI agent — one that runs continuously, works with your company-specific data, and handles a high-volume process end to end. NanoClaw is a custom AI agent platform. We configure, deploy, and operate it for your specific business problem, so you get a working system, not a software project.

*Typical commitment: Medium (3–10 days) to Large (ongoing)*

## How We Engage

Every engagement fits one of three sizes — designed to give you a clear sense of the commitment before you decide.

| Size | Duration | What it typically looks like |
|------|----------|------------------------------|
| **Small** | 0.5 – 2 days | Focused audit, assessment, or quick-win prototype |
| **Medium** | 3 – 10 days | A defined project with a clear deliverable |
| **Large** | 10+ days / ongoing | Full deployment, multi-workflow, or ongoing retainer |

Most clients start Small or Medium. The right size depends on what you're trying to accomplish and how fast you want to move.
