## About NewThink.ai

NewThink.ai is a Louisville-area applied AI firm built around one premise: there is no competitive advantage in AI alone. The advantage is how and where you use it.

Most organizations are stuck at the same place — they've seen the demos, experimented with the tools, and know AI matters. What they haven't found is a clear path from "this is interesting" to "this is working in our operation." That's the gap we fill.

---

## How We Work

We take what's sometimes called a forward-deployed approach: we work inside your operation, not outside it. That means sitting with your teams, understanding what they actually do, identifying where AI creates real value — and then building and deploying solutions around your specific workflows, your data, and your context.

Every engagement moves through three phases:

**Audit** — Map the workflows. Find the bottlenecks. Determine what should be automated, what should use code, and what should stay human. (Getting this last part right is important — and it's often what separates an effective AI deployment from an expensive failed experiment.)

**Build** — Prototype in your environment. Start small. Build something that works before building something big.

**Deploy** — Move to production carefully. Layer in capabilities only after the foundation holds.
